titanxxh2012-07-152026-08-22

The second thing my mother brought back from the United States... Completing the entire Small World collection is so difficult.

I have not read the rules yet, but apparently it can be combined with the original game. Fantastic—thirty races! Add three small expansions and there are more than forty races and over fifty powers... With tunnels included, could it possibly support a ten-player map?
