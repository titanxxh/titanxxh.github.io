[titanxxh's Essen 2014] Game Overview 4: Versailles

titanxxh2015-06-212026-08-23

The fourth game in the series is Versailles. Like the two titles titanxxh introduced previously, it was one of NSKN Games’ 2014 releases. titanxxh saw that its rulebook was only four pages long and immediately chose it for an introduction…


Versailles

Game Overview

Versailles is an innovative family game for two to five players. Acting as architects and interior designers, players cooperate to build a magnificent palace for King Louis XIV while competing for the monarch’s favor.
On each turn, a player moves one or more workers to another location, activating every one of their workers at that destination. Actions provide resources, allow players to build the mysterious palace, create attractive decorations for its interior and gardens, and learn new skills. When His Majesty arrives, he will judge which of his faithful servants has performed best.

Components

When playing for the first time, punch out the tokens carefully and sort the wooden pieces! Seriously.

  • Game board

The board contains the construction site in the center and action locations around it.

Top row: King’s Favor, Marble Quarry, Gold Mine, Architects’ Guild
Bottom row: Alchemists’ Guild, Construction Site, Lumber Mill, Workshop

  • The king

King Louis XIV comes to inspect the architects’ work.

  • 45 worker meeples

The wooden meeples represent workers. At the start of the game, the workers that players will use are placed in predetermined positions on the board.

  • 5 activation markers

An activation marker records the location a player activated most recently.

  • 20 technology/scoring markers

Technology/scoring markers track each player’s three technologies at the Alchemists’ Guild and their victory points.

  • 90 resource cubes

Gold is yellow, marble white, and wood brown. Small cubes represent one resource and large cubes five.

  • 40 palace tiles

Palace tiles depict the palace’s interior and exterior areas.

  • 30 decoration tokens

There are six kinds of decoration, and their supply is limited.

  • 10 double-move tokens

Double-move tokens record how many special movements a player has left.

Setup

  • General setup
    • Place the board in the center. Put wood at the Lumber Mill, marble at the Marble Quarry, and gold at the Gold Mine.
    • Shuffle the palace tiles into a face-down stack. Reveal five and place them at the Architects’ Guild.
    • Sort decoration tokens by type and put them next to the Workshop.
    • Choose the starting player: whoever most recently visited Versailles or Paris, or decide randomly.
    • Put the king on the first space of the king track.
  • Player setup
    • Each player takes all workers, technology markers, the activation marker, and the scoring marker in one color.
    • Each player takes the matching double-move tokens and places them face up.
    • Each player puts two workers on the new-worker spaces at King’s Favor and puts their scoring marker at the beginning of the score track.
    • At the Alchemists’ Guild, put one of the player’s technology markers on the first space of each technology row.
    • Set each player’s starting worker positions according to the table. Return unused workers to the box.

Gameplay

A game of Versailles consists of consecutive turns, beginning with the starting player and proceeding clockwise until the game ends. On a turn, a player moves one or more workers to another location and may activate that location’s action.

  • Moving workers

On their turn, a player must move at least one worker from one location to another. Workers must follow the arrows printed on the board. The player chooses either a regular move or a double move, following the rules below.

  • Regular move

A regular move lets the player move one worker a single step in the direction of an arrow.

  • Double move

A player may flip one double-move token to replace a regular move with a double move. They may either:

Move two workers from the same location one step each as regular moves.
Move one worker once as a regular move while skipping over one location.

  • Activation marker

Regardless of movement type, a player may never move to a location containing their own activation marker. After completing a move, they transfer that marker to the location just activated.

  • Activating a location

After moving, the player may choose whether to activate the location’s action. When activating a location, each worker may be used only once. (That is basically a tautology…)

  • Location benefits

Each location offers one or more benefits according to the number of the active player’s workers there. In general, more workers produce a greater benefit. Other players’ workers do not count.

- Marble Quarry

With one, two, or three workers, gain one, two, or three marble.

- Lumber Mill

With one, two, or three workers, gain one, two, or three wood.

- Gold Mine

With one, two, or three workers, gain one, two, or three gold.

- Architects' Guild

The Architects’ Guild offers several options. The number of workers determines how many options a player may choose, and the chosen actions may be performed in any order.

    1. Pay the worker cost shown to the left of one of the five displayed palace tiles to take that tile. Do not refill the display while tiles are being taken. A player may hold no more than four palace tiles at any time; on taking a fifth, immediately discard one.
    2. Spend one worker to remove one palace tile from the display, or two workers to remove three. Slide every remaining tile down afterward.
    3. Spend one worker to flip one double-move token face up. Regardless of how many workers a player has here, this action may be performed only once per turn.

After the player completes every desired action, slide the remaining palace tiles down and refill the display to five from the stack. If the stack is insufficient, shuffle the discarded palace tiles. If fewer than seven palace tiles are available in total, the game ends immediately.

- Workshop

Spend the indicated workers and resources to buy one or more decorations.

- Alchemists' Guild

Spend the indicated workers and resources to advance technologies.

- Construction Site

When activating the Construction Site, a player may place one or more palace tiles in the center of the board. Every tile must satisfy these conditions:

    1. Each palace tile shows a required number of workers. The player must have at least that many workers at the Construction Site.
    2. Pay the resources and decorations. Return resources to their locations; decorations remain permanently on the palace tile.
    3. Place the tile adjacent to an existing palace tile or along the edge of the Construction Site. Outdoor and indoor portions must match those of adjacent tiles, or placement is illegal.

After placing a tile, score the victory points printed on it. A player may place multiple palace tiles during a single activation.

- King's Favor

The king  
Whenever King's Favor is activated or skipped over, advance the king one space. If he reaches the final space, the game ends immediately.  
A player may activate King's Favor to recruit new workers or refresh double-move tokens:

    1. Recruiting a new worker requires two or three workers to activate, according to the printed cost. A newly recruited worker appears at King's Favor and cannot be used immediately. A player may recruit two new workers in one action, or recruit the more expensive worker first.
    2. Spend one worker to flip up to two double-move tokens face up.

Technologies

The Alchemists’ Guild contains three technology tracks. Technologies grant players special abilities.

  • Improved Resource Collection

This technology gives the player extra resources.

Level 1: whenever the player gains wood, gain one additional wood.
Level 2: whenever the player gains marble, gain one additional marble.
Level 3: whenever the player gains gold, gain one additional gold. Note that activating the Gold Mine with one worker also gains one gold.

  • Improved Resource Locations

This technology lets a player collect resources when another player does. The amount gained depends on how many of their workers occupy that location. At least one of their workers must be present to use the technology.

Level 1: whenever another player activates the Lumber Mill, this player may also activate it to gain wood.
Level 2: whenever another player activates the Marble Quarry, this player may also activate it to gain marble.
Level 3: whenever another player activates the Gold Mine, this player may also activate it to gain gold.

  • Improved Special Locations

This technology lets a player activate the Architects’ Guild, Workshop, or Construction Site when someone else activates it. Benefits depend on how many of their workers occupy that location, and at least one worker must be present. If several players have the technology, resolve their actions one at a time, beginning with the player to the active player’s left.

Level 1: whenever another player activates the Architects’ Guild, this player may also activate it.
Level 2: whenever another player activates the Workshop, this player may also activate it.
Level 3: whenever another player activates the Construction Site, this player may also activate it.

Game End

The game ends as soon as any of these conditions is met:

  • The Construction Site has no space remaining.
  • The king reaches the end of his track.
  • The palace-tile stack is empty and fewer than seven discarded palace tiles remain.

The game ends immediately when a condition is triggered. Even if the active player has unused workers, they may take no further actions.

Final Scoring

At the end of the game, players add these victory points:

  • Every three marble, three wood, or two gold are worth one point.
  • Every palace tile still in a player’s hand is worth one point.
  • Every decoration is worth three points.

The player with the most victory points wins. Tied players share the victory.
